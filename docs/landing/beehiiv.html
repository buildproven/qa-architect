The paid difference

Not just a report. A Release Receipt.

Pro binds the evidence to the exact change under review, so your team can see what passed, what blocks release, and what still needs verification.

PASSRequired checks and remediation evidence are complete.
BLOCKA known high-risk finding still needs attention.
INCOMPLETEEvidence is missing, stale, or unavailable.

A PASS means the configured checks and evidence are complete. It is not a security certification or a promise that an application has no bugs.

A freshness check does not authenticate the receipt producer or prove trusted execution. Independent authenticity requires a future signed or CI-attested receipt.
