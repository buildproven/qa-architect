Find the risk. Prove the release.

AI can build an app that works and still leave behind exposed secrets, broken permissions, or unsafe data access. QA Architect finds those mistakes, then helps you decide whether the exact change in front of you is ready to merge and ship.

Free finds the risk. Pro binds the checks, findings, and remediation evidence to one exact revision and reports PASS, BLOCK, or INCOMPLETE.

What Pro is for

Pro turns “we fixed it” into evidence.

When a change matters, a one-time scan is not enough. Pro checks the exact revision under review, records the required evidence, and makes missing or stale checks visible instead of calling them safe.

  • 01
    Review the exact change Changed-code findings, risk policy, and base/head identity stay bound to the candidate.
  • 02
    Verify the repair Supported Critical/High findings get inspectable packets, focused tests, and commit-bound proof.
  • 03
    Make the release call Ship Check reports PASS, BLOCK, or INCOMPLETE for the evidence that actually ran.
